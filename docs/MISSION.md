# Why Scam Bunker exists

Every day, people receive messages that are designed to deceive them: a job offer that asks for a "training fee", a bank text with a lookalike link, a QR code on a parking meter, a crypto "mentor" who texted the wrong number, a viral photo from a disaster that happened years ago, a video of a celebrity endorsing an investment they have never heard of. Most people have nobody to ask, and the tools professionals use are scattered, technical, or behind paywalls.

**Scam Bunker is a free, open-source place where anyone can check something suspicious and get a clear, honest answer, with the evidence, in under a minute.** It also gives fact-checkers, journalists, moderators and community organisers the deeper tools to investigate further.

## Who it is for

1. **Anyone with a suspicious message, link, image, number or claim.** Students, parents, small-business owners, community members: no training, no account, any phone.
2. **People who protect others.** Fact-checkers, journalists, trust-and-safety teams, university clubs and community leaders who verify material, track repeat offenders and teach verification.

We pay particular attention to places that are underserved by existing tools, for example Bangladesh and the wider South Asian region, where mobile-money fraud (bKash, Nagad, Rocket), fake job and visa offers, and Facebook and WhatsApp scams are common, and where content is often in Bangla or Banglish.

## What it checks

Not only one kind of scam. The goal is to cover the deception people actually meet:

- **Scams and fraud:** phishing, smishing (SMS), quishing (QR codes), investment and crypto fraud, pig-butchering, romance, job and task scams, fake shops, giveaways and prizes, advance-fee, impersonation of brands, banks, government and family members ("Hi Mum"), tech support, sextortion, recovery scams, mobile-money fraud.
- **Spam and manipulation:** engagement bait, comment rings, bot and coordinated networks, fake reviews and testimonials.
- **False information:** recycled or out-of-context images and video, manipulated or AI-generated media, false claims and fabricated quotes, misleading statistics.
- **Dangerous links:** lookalike and newly registered domains, redirect chains, shorteners, known phishing domains.

## Principles

1. **Answer first, evidence always.** Lead with a plain-language answer and what to do, and show every piece of evidence behind it.
2. **Leads, not verdicts about people.** We assess messages, links and media. We never infer motive and never label a person a criminal. Findings about people are leads for the organisations that can verify them.
3. **Honest about uncertainty.** Every signal is graded (proof, strong, moderate, weak). "We could not tell" is a valid answer.
4. **Private by default.** Analysis runs on the user's device wherever possible. Nothing is uploaded unless the user chooses to.
5. **Free and open.** MIT-licensed, zero dependencies, runs on a static website or a laptop, works without paid API keys.
6. **Teach, don't just tell.** Every result shows how to check it yourself, so people get better at spotting deception over time.
7. **Built on proven practice.** We follow the methods of established verification and fact-checking organisations (see [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md)).

## What success looks like

- A person with no training gets a correct, understandable answer and knows exactly what to do next.
- A fact-checker can take the same case further (provenance, timelines, offender links, a tamper-evident report) without switching tools.
- Communities reuse it: university clubs, NGOs and newsrooms run it, translate it, and add the scam patterns they see locally.

## What Scam Bunker is not

- Not a people-search or doxxing tool. Public profiles and public ledgers only.
- Not a replacement for police, banks or platform reporting. It tells you where to report.
- Not an oracle. It is a structured second opinion that shows its work.
