What you can submit

Image
Dated from EXIF, XMP, PNG and GPS timestamps. Checked for C2PA, IPTC source type and generator signatures. GPS, device serials and edit history are surfaced.
Text
A post, DM, email or comment thread. The scheme is classified (phishing, pig-butchering, job, giveaway, impersonation) and coordinated networks are flagged.
Link
Checked for typosquats, punycode, brand-in-subdomain tricks, shorteners, redirects and tracking.

Privacy

Images and text are analysed in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on the agent team or hand the case to Claude. Case files and the offender index stay in this browser's local storage.
